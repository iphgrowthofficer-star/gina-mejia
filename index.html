About

A career built in the space between a carrier and its partners.

I started in 2001 leading the Chicago and Cleveland markets for The Mobile Solution. The company began as a Cingular dealer and went on to become T-Mobile's first exclusive dealer, and its largest, starting back in the VoiceStream days. In 2007 I joined T-Mobile and earned seven internal promotions, from account development and event programs to sales and operations, district leadership and national account management.

Because I have worked for a dealer, with dealers, and in company-owned stores, I understand what each side is up against. Today I'm the person both dealer ownership and T-Mobile headquarters call when something needs to move, whether that's a new national program, a growth plan or a problem that's costing stores sales.

I do my best work where relationships, operational discipline and people development decide the outcome: leading national accounts, running multi-location operations and building the leaders who keep stores performing.
